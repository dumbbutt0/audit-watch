## Summary
The per-holder maxSellPercent cap in VestingManager.listVesting is enforced as require(sold <= bought + maxSellPercent*(currentAlloc+sold-bought)/BASE). `completePurchase` credits `bought += amount` unconditionally (no buyer!=seller guard in Marketplace._validatePurchase), so a holder who buys their OWN public listing inflates `bought`, which is added at 100% to sellLimit while (currentAlloc+sold-bought) stays = original allocation. Repeated self-buy cycles (fee cost only) lift the cap without bound, letting the holder sell more than maxSellPercent of their original locked allocation to an external buyer.
