# Triage — AC-003

**Status:** submit  ·  **Severity:** Low

**ICE:** I=Low · C=Confirmed · E=Conditional

## Decision reason
Confirmed: LibTractor._domainSeparatorV4 uses BLUEPRINT_TYPE_HASH as the domain typehash instead of the defined EIP712_TYPE_HASH, producing a non-standard domain separator. No auth bypass/replay results: the same non-standard separator is used by both signer-recovery and verifier, chainId is included, and a per-blueprint nonce with maxNonce bounds replay. Impact is standards/interoperability: signatures from standard EIP-712 tooling won't verify (self-DoS of legitimate signers). Low. Not safety-net (no protocol-protection role); valid Low correctness finding.

## Follow-up packet
Reachable: any standards-compliant signer producing a Tractor blueprint signature. No privileged keys. State: signature fails to verify against the non-standard separator. Guards fail: wrong typehash constant. Scope: EIP-712 interoperability. Low.
