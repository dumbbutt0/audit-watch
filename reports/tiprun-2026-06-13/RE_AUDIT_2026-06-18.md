**Scope:** entire `src/` — 63 Solidity files, ~4,882 LOC, 17 transaction types, 10 plugins (core / perp /

**Method:** audit-watch pipeline as audit `tiprun-2026-06-18` — Step-0 deep analysis (12 invariants, 8

Findings were re-derived **independently** (specialists were not pre-fed the prior hunt's results), then
reconciled.

> **The single fact that governs scope.** The perp risk/margin/liquidation engine is live only when the
> `CAP_MARGIN_CHECK` capability is registered to the real `Risk` contract. When it is not, `PositionManager.
> _marginCheck()` falls back to `NullMarginCheck` (a no-op) and the oracle is never read. The live
> predict/spot deployment runs that way (the reason the prior F-2 was ruled out of scope). So every
> **perp-only** finding below is a real code defect that is **out of scope unless the target deployment wires
> the perp `Risk` engine.** Confirm `CAP_MARGIN_CHECK` wiring with the program — it decides F-1/F-2/M-2/M-3 and
> the new PC-01 at once. **Core-shared** and **predict/spot** findings are reachable on the live deployment.

## 2. NEW findings (not in the prior hunt)

### N-1 (High, core-shared, **LIVE**) — Permission-model escalation: `ALLOW_ADMIN` mintable at registration + admin-account not bound
Corroborated independently by **two** specialists (access_control AC-001/AC-002, behavioral BSA-001) and
verified by hand. Two composable on-chain gaps:
- **(a)** `RegisterAccountTransLib.process` (lines 70-82) forwards a registering owner's `SignerInit[]`
  permission masks **verbatim** into `SignerManager.addSignerWithPermissions`, which stores a non-owner
  signer's mask as-is — including `ALLOW_ADMIN` (`SignerManager.sol:113-115`; the `_restrictAdminBit` rule was
  removed). Registration has **no** delegation gate (unlike the in-flight `processAddSignersWithPermissions`).
  So an owner who does **not** hold `ALLOW_ADMIN` can register an account with a session key that does.
- **(b)** `PermissionsTransLib.processAdminSetSignerPermissions` (lines 158-165) checks `ALLOW_ADMIN` on the
  **caller-supplied** `txData.adminAccountId`, never binding it to canonical `SignerManager.adminAccountId()`,
  then writes an arbitrary `mask` on any `targetAccountId`.
- **Chain:** register account A with an `ALLOW_ADMIN` session key → that key signs `ADMIN_SET(adminAccountId=A,
  target=victim, mask=ALLOW_TRANSFER|ALLOW_WITHDRAW)` → move/withdraw the victim's collateral.
- **Reachability:** core-shared (account/permission system — live on **every** profile, not perp-gated).
  **Gated only by the off-chain operator** (all txs flow through `onlyTxBatcher`); there is **no on-chain gate**.
  The prior hunt classified `ALLOW_ADMIN` as "design, no finding" — gap (b) (admin account not bound to the
  canonical value) is a clear bug regardless of the multi-admin design intent.
- **Fix:** (1) bind `processAdminSetSignerPermissions` to `signerManager.adminAccountId()`; (2) apply the
  owner-holds-the-bit delegation gate to registration-time masks (or strip `ALLOW_ADMIN` at registration).

### N-2 (Medium, perp/deployment-config) — Capability registry silently degrades to no-op checks
`PC-01` (protocol_context). `registerPlugin` never enforces that a `CAP_MARGIN_CHECK` / `CAP_BALANCE_CHECK`
provider exists (no plugin declares `requiredCapabilities`); unset → `NullMarginCheck` / `NullBalanceCheck`
no-ops. A perp deployment with an unwired/temporarily-unregistered `CAP_MARGIN_CHECK` accepts undercollateralized
trades/withdrawals → insolvency. This is the **root determinant** of every margin-bypass finding's reachability.
Fix: enforce required capability providers at registration; refuse to process margin-gated txTypes without a real
provider.

### N-3 (Medium, perp-only) — int256 overflow in risk products reverts before the boundary check
`DOS-002` / `AR-001` / `AR-002`. `riskRep = absAmount * oraclePrice * riskFactor` (an FXP² triple product,
`Risk.sol:66-67`) and the `getRiskFactor` notional product are computed under checked math **before**
`getAccountPositionStatusWithBoundaryCheck` can range-revert gracefully — so an extreme notional panics
(overflow) on every risk-gated path. A distinct *mechanism* for the same freeze/un-liquidatable impact class as
F-2 (the in-code "Safe from overflow" comment is wrong about ordering).

### N-4 (Medium, predict/spot **LIVE**) — Settlement mints collateral with no conservation/price constraint
`ECON-002` (sharpens prior L-1/L-4). `SettlementTransLib.process` mints `collateral = syntheticBalance *
settlementPrice` per account with **no aggregate YES/NO conservation, no signature/oracle binding, and raw
(non-FXP_32) scale**, where `settlementPrice` is an arbitrary operator input in `[0, int128.max]`. On the **live
predict** path, settlement solvency rests entirely on operator honesty; an FXP-scaled price over-credits by 2³².
Operator-trusted, but the largest unconstrained-authority surface on the live deployment.

## 4. Reviewed and sound (no finding)
Trade conservation + fairness (signature-bounded; synthetic & collateral conserve across both legs + fee
account); funding apply-once (cached-index idempotent, monotonic timestamp guard — the very guard the Oracle
lacks); permission **delegation** in the in-flight paths (grant-only-what-you-hold); nonce single-use per signer
(no cross-account replay); `SyntheticPositionLib` bit-packing (no mask collisions); EIP-712 domain binding
(chainId + verifyingContract); initializer/storage hygiene (every upgradeable contract has
`_disableInitializers()` + `initializer`; Transparent proxies, no UUPS auth gap); reentrancy (only EOA-reachable
external call is the owner-set deposit token; dispatch chain fully gated).

## 5. Honest caveats
- **Trust model.** The exchange is off-chain-match; every state change flows through the trusted `txBatcher`.
  Most findings are therefore operator-trust-dependent — but several are *missing on-chain safety invariants*
  that should not rest solely on off-chain validation (notably N-1's gap (b) and the F-1/F-2 margin invariants).
- **Perp reachability.** F-1, F-2, M-2, M-3, N-2, N-3 are perp-only and out of scope on a predict/spot
  (`NullMarginCheck`) deployment — the same reason F-2 was previously ruled OOS. **N-1 (escalation) and N-4
  (settlement) are live on predict/spot** and do not depend on the perp engine.
- **PoCs.** High findings (LOG-001, DOS-001, BSA-001) are `poc_required` and sit in `human_pending` in the
  canonical records — they are not "READY" until a PoC proves the claimed impact (the pipeline's precision
  rule). F-1/F-2/M-3 can reuse the prior harness PoCs; **N-1 needs a new PoC** (register→admin-set→drain).
- This re-audit found real bugs in source; the largest *new* result is **N-1**, a live, core-shared
  authorization gap the prior hunt missed by treating `ALLOW_ADMIN` as purely "by design."
